Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make my first song?
Tap New Song, set a tempo (snap your fingers, tap along, or drag the slider), then sing or hum. Eight to ten seconds is plenty to start. Stop recording and Song Baby shows your notes, the keys that fit, and chords to go with them.
Why can't Song Baby hear me?
Song Baby needs microphone access. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn on Song Baby. Sing a little closer to the phone in a quiet room for the cleanest notes.
How do I change the key or a chord?
Tap any suggested key while your song plays to hear it in that key. To change a single chord, press and hold its bar and pick another one that fits.
How do I get my song into Logic Pro or GarageBand?
Open the song and tap Export MIDI. Send the file to your Mac with AirDrop or save it to Files, then open it in Logic Pro, GarageBand or any MIDI-compatible app.
What does the unlock include?
Your first 3 songs are free. The one-time Unlock Song Baby purchase lets you keep saving new songs forever. There's no subscription.
I got a new iPhone. How do I restore my purchase?
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is analyzed and stored on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.
