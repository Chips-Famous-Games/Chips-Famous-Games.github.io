Privacy Policy
Effective date: August 16, 2026
The short version: Free Climb doesn't collect your data.

No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking, no analytics. Your game progress lives on your device and nowhere else.

What we collect

Nothing. Free Climb does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no user accounts, does not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts, and contains no analytics or tracking software of any kind.

Where your progress lives

Your points, unlocked levels, and settings are saved locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device, and we have no ability to see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third parties

Free Climb does not share data with third parties, because it has no data to share. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or social media SDKs.

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Free Climb is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Free Climb ever changes how it handles data — for example, by adding optional advertising or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match. The current policy always describes the current version of the app.

Questions? Get in touch

If you have any questions about this policy, email freeclimbsupport@gmail.com — Free Climb is made by one person, and every message gets read.
