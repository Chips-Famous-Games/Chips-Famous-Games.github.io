Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Tap a glowing hold to move a hand there and climb higher. Keep an eye on your energy bar — big holds are easy to hang on to, tiny ones tire you out fast. Reach the top before your energy runs out. The in-game tutorial (the Belay Class) walks you through everything in about a minute.
Why do I keep falling?
A few usual suspects: your energy ran out mid-wall (grab snack bars to refill it), you moved too fast on a small, slippery hold (the chalk bag goodie prevents slips), or you leapt for a faraway hold and missed — a miss on a big jump is always a fall. That one's on purpose. Be bold at your own risk!
How do I unlock new levels and places?
Points! Every climb you finish banks points toward your total, and your total unlocks harder levels and new places automatically — nothing is ever spent or taken away. Finish a wall after collecting every goodie on it and your points for that climb are doubled.
Is my progress saved?
Yes — your points and unlocks are saved on your device automatically. No account or sign-up is needed. Just don't delete the app, as removing it also removes its saved data.
I found a bug. What should I tell you?
Email me with your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened right before the problem. A screenshot or screen recording is gold. I fix bugs in the order they come in.
Can I suggest a feature or a new location?
Absolutely — ideas are always welcome. More places to climb are already on the way, and player suggestions help decide what comes next.
